# 🎨 New Features Added!

Your Personal Diary just got a MAJOR upgrade! Here's everything new:

---

## 🎨 Beautiful Color Themes

### Custom Colors for Each Entry
- Choose from 6 preset colors or pick any color you want
- Each entry can have its own unique color theme
- Colors appear in the sidebar for easy visual identification

**Preset Colors:**
- 💜 Purple (Default)
- 💚 Green
- 🧡 Orange
- ❤️ Red
- 💜 Violet
- 💗 Pink

**How to use:**
1. Create or edit an entry
2. Click on a color preset or use the color picker
3. Save your entry
4. See the colored border in the sidebar!

---

## 📂 Categories

### Organize Your Entries
Now you can categorize your diary entries:

- 📝 **Personal** - Personal thoughts and reflections
- 💼 **Work** - Work-related notes and ideas
- ✈️ **Travel** - Travel experiences and memories
- 💪 **Health** - Health, fitness, and wellness
- 🎯 **Goals** - Goals, plans, and achievements
- 🙏 **Gratitude** - Things you're grateful for

**How to use:**
1. Select a category when creating/editing an entry
2. Filter entries by category in the sidebar
3. See category badges on each entry

## 😊 Mood Tracking

### Track Your Emotions
Record how you felt when writing each entry:

- 😊 **Happy** - Feeling good and positive
- 😢 **Sad** - Feeling down or melancholic
- 🤩 **Excited** - Feeling energized and enthusiastic
- 😰 **Anxious** - Feeling worried or stressed
- 😐 **Neutral** - Feeling calm and balanced

**How to use:**
1. Select your mood when creating/editing an entry
2. Filter entries by mood in the sidebar
3. See mood emojis on each entry
4. Track your emotional patterns over time

---

## ⭐ Favorites

### Mark Important Entries
Star your most important or memorable entries:

- Mark entries as favorites with one click
- Filter to show only favorite entries
- Favorites appear with a ⭐ icon in the sidebar

**How to use:**
1. Click the "⭐ Mark as Favorite" checkbox
2. Save your entry
3. Click "⭐ Favorites" filter to see only starred entries

---

## 🔍 Advanced Filtering

### Find Entries Faster
Multiple ways to filter your entries:

1. **Search by Tag** - Find entries with specific tags
2. **Filter by Category** - Show only work, travel, etc.
3. **Filter by Mood** - Find entries by how you felt
4. **Favorites Only** - Show only starred entries
5. **Date Range** - Filter by date range
6. **Combine Filters** - Use multiple filters together!

**How to use:**
1. Use the search section in the sidebar
2. Select your filters
3. Click "Apply Filters"
4. Click "Clear All" to reset

## 🎯 How to Use New Features

### Creating a Colorful Entry:

1. Click **"+ New Entry"**
2. Add a title: "My Amazing Day"
3. Select category: **✈️ Travel**
4. Select mood: **🤩 Excited**
5. Pick a color: **🧡 Orange**
6. Add tags: "vacation, beach, fun"
7. Check **⭐ Mark as Favorite**
8. Write your entry
9. Click **💾 Save**

### Finding Entries:

**Example 1: Find all work entries**
- Select category: **💼 Work**
- Click "Apply Filters"

**Example 2: Find happy travel memories**
- Select category: **✈️ Travel**
- Select mood: **😊 Happy**
- Click "Apply Filters"

**Example 3: Find favorite entries from last month**
- Click **⭐ Favorites** button
- Set date range
- Click "Apply Filters"
